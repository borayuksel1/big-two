2♠︎Big Two
Setting up the table

How we play

  • Cards rank 3 (lowest) up to A, then 2 is the highest card.
  • Suits rank ♣︎ clubs < ♦︎ diamonds < ♥︎ hearts < ♠︎ spades. Same rank? The higher suit wins.
  • Two to eight players. With four or more, the whole deck is dealt one card at a time, so some players may get one extra card. With two or three, everyone gets 13.
  • Whoever holds the 3♣︎ leads first, and that first play has to include it.
  • Tap cards to pick them, then press Play, or drag a card (or your picked cards) up onto the table.
  • Play a single, a pair, three of a kind, four of a kind or a five-card hand. To follow, play the same number of cards and beat what's on the table.
  • Each hand is dealt in a random order, then you get 30 seconds to arrange your cards. Drag them into any order, or tap By rank or By suit.
  • You get 20 seconds per turn. Run out and you pass automatically (or, if you're leading, your lowest card is played).
  • Pass and you're out until someone wins the round. When everyone else has passed, the last player to play leads anything.
  • There are no winners, only a loser. Get rid of all your cards and you're safe. Everyone else keeps playing until one person is left holding cards.
  • The loser takes 1 penalty point per card still in hand and shuffles the next hand.

Five-card hands, weakest to strongest:

StraightStraight flush

Four of a kind is played as just the four cards, never with a fifth. Like a pair or a triple, it can only be beaten by a higher four of a kind.

  • Straights run 3-4-5-6-7 up to 10-J-Q-K-A. No 2s in straights.
  • No flushes: five cards of one suit only count if they also make a straight. No full houses, and four of a kind can't be padded with a fifth card. Straights and straight flushes compare by their top card.
  • Every card dealt has a 1 in 8192 chance to be shiny: black paper, blue and silver ink. It plays exactly like a normal card. It's just for looks.
  • Pick your table under Table: the classic red felt, or Bodrum: the teak table on the deck, bougainvillea everywhere, drinks and nuts on the table. Each player picks their own.
  • No money and no betting. Points only, for bragging rights.
